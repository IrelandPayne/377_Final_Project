The Purpose

Economic data is often shown in large tables, reports, and news articles that can be difficult to understand. The Global Economic Trends Dashboard is designed to make that information easier to read by turning major economic indicators into simple charts, summaries, and comparisons.

Users can explore indicators such as GDP, inflation, unemployment, and life expectancy across different countries. This dashboard was designed to help students, teachers, researchers, non-profits, and everyday users better understand how countries are changing over time.

Compare Countries

The Compare page allows users to view two countries side-by-side using the same economic indicator. This makes it easier to understand how countries differ in areas such as GDP, GDP per capita, inflation, unemployment, and life expectancy.
