The Purpose

Economic data such as GDP, inflation, unemployment, and life expectancy are often reported in the news. Still, most people do not fully understand what these numbers mean, how they compare, how they change, and their long-term effects. Many people find economic data confusing because it is often presented in large tables or reports with complex explanations.

The goal of this website is to create a simple, interactive dashboard that turns global economic data into concise summaries and comparisons. Click around to explore indicators such as GDP, inflation, unemployment, and life expectancy across different countries.
