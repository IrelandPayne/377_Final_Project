Help Page

Need help? Take a look at our definitions below to better understand our data.

Navigation

Use the navigation bar at the top of the page to move between the Home, Compare, Country, About, and Help pages.

Compare Page

The Compare page allows users to compare two countries using one economic indicator.

First, select two countries from the dropdown menus. Then, select an indicator such as GDP, inflation, unemployment, or life expectancy. Finally, click the compare button to generate a chart comparing both countries over time.

Country Page

The Country page allows users to analyze one country in more detail.

Select a country and indicator, then click the button to view the results. The page displays important statistics such as:

Earliest Value: The oldest available value.

Latest Value: The most recent available value.

Percent Change: The overall increase or decrease over time.

Highest Value: The year with the largest recorded value.

Lowest Value: The year with the smallest recorded value.

Trend Direction: Whether the indicator generally increased or decreased over time.

Economic Definitions

GDP: GDP, or Gross Domestic Product, measures the total value of goods and services produced by a country.

Inflation: Inflation measures how much prices increase over time.

Unemployment: Unemployment measures the percentage of people who are looking for work but do not have a job.

Life Expectancy: Life expectancy measures the average number of years a person is expected to live.
